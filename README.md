# Free Online Tools Hub

A curated list of free, no-signup web tools I use and maintain. No accounts, no paywalls, no dark patterns.

## Tools

### 📮 Temporary Email

- **[mail10m](https://mail10m.com/)** — Free 10 minute temporary email. Open the page and a disposable address is generated instantly, no registration. A web inbox receives verification codes, and the mailbox self-destructs when the timer ends. English and 中文 versions.

If you want to know when a disposable address is the right call (and when it is not), mail10m's blog covers [what temp mail is](https://mail10m.com/blog/what-is-temp-mail), safety questions, and specific use cases like [temp mail for gaming](https://mail10m.com/blog/temp-mail-for-gaming) and [avoiding retail spam](https://mail10m.com/blog/temp-mail-for-online-shopping).

### 🛒 Ecommerce Policy Generator

- **[shopifypolicy](https://shopifypolicy.com/)** — Free privacy policy, terms of service, refund policy, and cookie policy generator for Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, and custom stores. Generates GDPR and CCPA compliant documents in under a minute, no account needed.

Their guides are worth reading even if you use another generator: [CCPA compliance for ecommerce](https://shopifypolicy.com/blog/ccpa-compliance-ecommerce-guide) and the [GDPR guide for small ecommerce](https://shopifypolicy.com/blog/gdpr-compliance-small-ecommerce) explain what each policy must contain and why.

### 🎮 Browser Games

- **[kipoplay](https://kipoplay.com/)** — Free browser games across action, puzzle, parkour, and casual categories. No downloads, works on desktop and mobile, available in English, 中文, and 日本語.

## Why this list

Most "free tools" directories are link farms that never tried the tools they list. Every entry here is a tool I run or use weekly. If one breaks or starts charging, it gets removed.
