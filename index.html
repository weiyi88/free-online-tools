Free Online Tools

Curated list — no accounts, no paywalls, no dark patterns. Every tool below is one I run or use weekly.

Temporary email

  • mail10m — free 10 minute temporary email. Open the page and a disposable address is generated instantly, no registration. A web inbox receives verification codes, and the mailbox self-destructs when the timer ends. English and 中文 versions.

Wondering when a disposable address is the right call? mail10m's blog covers what temp mail is, safety questions, and use cases like temp mail for gaming and avoiding retail spam.

Ecommerce policy generator

  • shopifypolicy — free privacy policy, terms of service, refund policy, and cookie policy generator for Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, and custom stores. Generates GDPR and CCPA compliant documents in under a minute, no account needed.

Their guides are worth reading even if you use another generator: CCPA compliance for ecommerce and the GDPR guide for small ecommerce explain what each policy must contain.

Browser games

  • kipoplay — free browser games across action, puzzle, parkour, and casual categories. No downloads, works on desktop and mobile, available in English, 中文, and 日本語.

Quick utilities (new additions for 2026)

  • CoinFlipSim — a coin flip simulator with a proper 3D animation, sound, and live session stats. Supports batch flips of 10, 100, or 1000 for probability experiments. Runs entirely in the browser.
  • WaterIntakeCalc — daily water intake calculator. Enter weight, sex, exercise, and climate; get a personal hydration target plus reference charts for runners, gym-goers, pregnancy, and kids.
  • ClickTally — tally counter and click counter with multiple named counters, CPS test, and CSV export. Knitting rows, reps, pitches, inventory — one tab handles them all.
  • CaseCraft — text case converter: upper, lower, title, sentence, camel, snake, alternating case, plus line-break removers and a character counter.
  • CursiveText — cursive and fancy text generator. Copy-paste script, gothic, bubble, and Old English fonts for bios and usernames on platforms that only allow plain text.
  • ComboEmoji — aesthetic emoji combos for captions and bios: cute, sad, love, birthday, friendship sets, ready to copy in one tap.
  • ConvertPlus — unit converter covering temperature (C↔F), weight, length, and volume, with 30+ dedicated pages for exact values like 180°C to °F and 350°F to °C.
  • RandomNames — 27 name generators: random full names, surnames by letter, usernames for Instagram/TikTok/Discord, fantasy and character names, plus a name picker wheel.
  • QuickTimer — online countdown timers and stopwatch. Dedicated pages from 1 to 60 minutes with fullscreen mode, wake lock, and a synthesized end beep.
  • DobCalc — age calculator that breaks your age down to days, hours, and minutes, with next-birthday countdown and Korean/Chinese age modes.
